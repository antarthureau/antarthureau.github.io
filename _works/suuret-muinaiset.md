Interactive audiovisual system for *Suuret muinaiset päiväunilla*, a long-term installation in Turku made with Joel Stenroos and Jesse Kitinoja.

The installation has three units, each one a creature: LONG, SMALL and SEASHELL. Each unit runs on a Teensy 4.0, plays its own audio file through its own amplifier and speaker, and drives its LEDs from that audio signal. LONG leads and keeps time for the other two.
