I'm a Bergen-based multimedia designer, engineer and consultant with a background in music, video, electronic and digital media, and interaction design. I advocate for open science and free technologies, and over the years I've worked with many artists and cultural institutions on all kinds of projects.

I've offered consulting services since 2015, specialising in creative tech systems, education and, more broadly, the use of technology in art.

Outside work I enjoy cooking and woodworking, practise various sports, and I'm enrolled in a bachelor's programme in cognitive science.

## at the university {#university}

I'm head engineer (overingeniør) for music and performance at the [Faculty of Fine Art, Music and Design](https://kmd.uib.no/en/frontpage) (KMD), University of Bergen. My work there covers:

- **Instrument manager at the Grieg Academy**: purchasing and repair of instruments and equipment, investments and inventory.
- **Performance and stage workshop at the Art Academy**: running and maintaining the workshop, and teaching stage technology, electronics and programming for time-based art.
- **Technical advice for research**: technology in KMD's research projects such as ARIIS, Sounding Philosophy, Sound as Illustration and NorARTNet, support for individual research fellows, and upgrading the faculty's digital research infrastructure.
- **Tredjepartsportalen** contact at KMD.

From 2019 to 2023 I was head engineer of the electronic and digital media workshop. There I built a complete media production environment and an electronics lab, covering everything from traditional AV studios to experimental, custom work with electronic and digital media. The workshop became central to many students' work and to the faculty's research projects.

The courses I teach are listed on the [teaching]({{ '/teaching/' | relative_url }}) page.
